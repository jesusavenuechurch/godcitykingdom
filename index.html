The Prophet

Sicelo Dlamini

Sicelo Dlamini is a sought-after prophetic voice among the younger generation as well as seasoned believers and ministers. He is widely recognized for his deep revelation knowledge, biblically sound doctrine, and profound teaching ministry. His message strongly emphasizes godly integrity and spiritual maturity.

"The Kingdom of God is not a religion — it is a reality to be lived, demonstrated, and declared."

His heart and divine assignment are to see the Body of Christ grow into the fullness of Christ, with a particular passion for the restoration of integrity within the prophetic ministry.

✦

First Lady

Nozipho Dlamini

Nozipho Dlamini is a prophet in the marketplace — a woman whose assignment extends far beyond the walls of the church into every sphere of society. She stands as a co-leader of God's City Kingdom Centre, walking alongside Sicelo Dlamini in vision, faith, and purpose.

"A woman of steadfast grace, she does not merely support the vision — she carries it."

As a mother, a wife, and a leader, Nozipho models the path of a woman who walks in both strength and grace. Her life is a living testimony to what God can do through a surrendered heart. She continues to inspire women across this generation to step boldly into their God-given identity and calling.

✝
Blessed With Three Children Sikhuselo, Buhlebayo & Hlokoma

The Vision

Pastor & Visionary

Sicelo Dlamini serves as the pastor and visionary of God's City Kingdom Centre — a growing ministry that has expanded into multiple campuses known as Word Centres across Eswatini.

Through visionary leadership and sound doctrine, the ministry continues to impact lives and equip believers toward spiritual maturity and kingdom effectiveness. His leadership raises up a people who carry the presence of God in every sphere of life.

His heart is to see the church be the church — a city set on a hill, shining as a beacon of truth, transformation, and the glory of God to all nations.
