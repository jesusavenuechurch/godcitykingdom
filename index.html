The Prophet

Prophet
Sicelo Dlamini

Prophet Sicelo Dlamini is a man called and ordained by God to bring transformation to generations and nations. His ministry is rooted in the prophetic, anchored in prayer, and driven by a deep love for people and the Kingdom of God.

"The Kingdom of God is not a religion — it is a reality to be lived, demonstrated, and declared."

As the founder of God's City Kingdom Centre, Prophet Dlamini leads with vision, boldness, and grace — raising up a people who carry the presence of God in every sphere of life. His heart is to see the church be the church — a city set on a hill.
