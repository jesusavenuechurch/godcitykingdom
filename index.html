Who we are

A city set on a hill

God's City Kingdom Centre is led by Prophet Sicelo and Nozipho Dlamini. From our main church in Ngwenya, the ministry has grown into six Word Centres across Eswatini.

Our vision is for the church to be the church: people who carry God's presence into every part of life, grow to maturity in Christ, and shine like a city on a hill, a light of truth and transformation to the nations.

Rooted in the WordSound doctrine and teaching you can live by.

Our senior leaders

Sicelo & Nozipho Dlamini

Prophet Sicelo and First Lady Nozipho Dlamini lead God's City together, walking side by side in vision, faith and purpose.

Prophet Sicelo is the pastor and visionary of God's City, and a prophetic voice trusted by young people and seasoned ministers alike. He is known for deep revelation, sound doctrine and strong teaching, with an emphasis on integrity and spiritual maturity. His heart is to see the Body of Christ grow into the fullness of Christ, and to see integrity restored in prophetic ministry.

Nozipho is a prophet in the marketplace, whose calling reaches beyond the church into every sphere of society. As a mother, wife and leader, she inspires women to step boldly into their God-given identity and calling.

They have three children: Sikhuselo, Buhlebayo and Hlokoma.

"The Kingdom of God is not a religion. It is a reality to be lived, demonstrated and declared."Prophet Sicelo Dlamini
