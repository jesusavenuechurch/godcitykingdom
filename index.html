Our senior leaders

Sicelo & Nozipho Dlamini

They lead God's City together, and have three children: Sikhuselo, Buhlebayo and Hlokoma.

The ProphetSicelo Dlamini

Pastor and visionary of God's City. He is known for sound teaching from the Bible, with an emphasis on integrity and spiritual maturity, and has a real passion for integrity in prophetic ministry.

First LadyNozipho Dlamini

Co-leader of the church and a prophet in the marketplace. She takes faith into business and everyday life, and encourages women to step into their calling.

Beyond Sunday

Our ministries

The work of God's City reaches beyond our own walls: on the radio, in a prophetic school, and through a network of churches.

Radio ministry

The Table of the Finishing Man

A weekly programme of teaching on Voice of God, our local Christian station, on 95, 97 and 101 MHz.

Prophetic school

Equipping the saints

Training for believers and leaders, held in different venues in Eswatini and beyond, to help the church grow to maturity.

Ministry network

The Table of the Finishing Man

A growing network of churches under the apostolic covering of Prophet Sicelo Dlamini. Each church stays independent, united in vision and doctrine.

Grace Portal FM

Our online radio, live and on demand, wherever you are.
